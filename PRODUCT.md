# Product

## Register

product

## Users

This is for people who read local novels on a desktop and want the reader to stay quiet, lightweight, and easy to hide. They usually want to pick a folder, continue where they left off, read TXT or EPUB files, and switch between normal and transparent reading with minimal friction.

## Product Purpose

The product is a local novel reader focused on low-interruption reading. Success means the user can find a book, read comfortably, recover progress, navigate chapters when needed, and reduce the interface to floating readable text in transparent mode.

## Brand Personality

Low-profile, lightweight, eye-friendly. The interface should feel discreet and calm, with controls available when needed and absent when they are not. The reading surface should carry the experience; surrounding chrome should stay restrained.

## Anti-references

Do not make this feel like a complex library-management app, a commercial reading platform, or a feature-heavy bookshelf product. The app should not optimize for cataloging, recommendations, storefront behavior, social layers, or decorative UI.

Do not preserve current layout decisions when they conflict with better reading-product practice. Existing structure is evidence, not a constraint: for example, the half-collapsed right outline can be removed if it does not serve the reading workflow.

## Design Principles

1. Keep reading first. Every surface should protect long-form text readability before adding navigation, metadata, or controls.
2. Let chrome disappear. The app should reduce visual weight aggressively when the user is reading, especially in transparent mode.
3. Keep navigation available, not dominant. File lists, chapter selectors, and outlines should support orientation without competing with the body text.
4. Prefer lightweight structure over library complexity. Avoid patterns that imply account systems, collections, commerce, or heavy document management.
5. Redesign from best practice when needed. Future work should follow the impeccable product UI guidance and the confirmed product purpose, not copy current rough layouts by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support light and dark themes, reduce distraction, and keep the body text readable in transparent mode. Future interface work should target WCAG AA contrast for normal UI text and maintain a clear focus state for keyboard users. Motion should be restrained, state-based, and safe for reduced-motion users.
